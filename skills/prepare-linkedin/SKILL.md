name: prepare-linkedin
description: Resolve an exact LinkedIn job through its authenticated Apply route, or prepare its observed Easy Apply modal with verified candidate answers.
---

Use the registered Browser Use CLI, default daemon and shared browser lane.
The candidate explicitly authorized the existing signed-in LinkedIn session.
Open or reuse the exact numeric job URL; profile pages and search-result links
do not establish application ownership. Preserve unrelated tabs and drafts.

For external Apply, inspect the exact job's accessible Apply control and follow
it with a native click. LinkedIn safety links may open a new tab and finish a
second redirect; wait for the actual employer URL before classifying the board.
Reuse an existing exact employer job tab when the currently observed Apply href
has the same verified job identity. Send the actual destination through the
source resolver, then use that board's planning skill and adapter.

For Easy Apply, treat the modal as a separate application wizard. Observe only
controls owned by the exact job's modal. Re-observe after Next/Review and account
for conditional questions, retained dropdown selections and uploaded documents.
LinkedIn's saved profile/resume suggestions do not supersede the role-specific
approved booklet or source PDF. A selected filename alone does not verify bytes.
Phone fields with a separate country selector use the approved national number.
New factual questions and unresolved verification remain handoffs.

Use the shared final-action gate only when the Easy Apply adapter has live
ownership, document, final-review and confirmation evidence. Until that adapter
is implemented and validated, record an actionable operational handoff; do not
misroute the modal to an external ATS or report it submitted.

Inactive tabs have silently ignored native Apply clicks in live validation.
Only after observing that failure, activate this owned job and retry navigation
once. Never retry a terminal submission with uncertain outcome.
